Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What this app is

"Inside Your Scan" is a private publishing tool. Only the owner of the Inside Your Scan YouTube channel uses it, to upload that channel's own educational videos. It is not offered to the public.

Google and YouTube data

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel (scope youtube.upload);
  • read the channel's name to confirm it is signed in to the right channel (scope youtube.readonly).

It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or other YouTube users. The only stored credential is the owner's sign-in token, which is kept privately and used for nothing else.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers of the channel

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. When you watch videos on YouTube, Google's own privacy policy applies.

Revoking access

The owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, use the contact details on the Inside Your Scan YouTube channel's About page.
